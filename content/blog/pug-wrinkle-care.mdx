Wrinkles are adorable. They also trap… everything. Here’s a porch-friend routine that keeps your pug fresh in minutes.

- Fragrance-free wipes
- Soft cotton pads
- A vet-approved drying balm

**Tip:** Be gentle, never harsh — you’re cleaning folds, not scrubbing pots.
